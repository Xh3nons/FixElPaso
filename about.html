Fix El Paso · El Paso, TX

Stronger cities start with
engaged residents.

🗺️
Our Mission

To strengthen communication between El Pasoans and local government by providing a community-driven platform for documenting and raising awareness of infrastructure concerns throughout El Paso.

🏙️
Our Vision

An El Paso where residents and government work together to improve our city and build stronger communities — through visibility, participation, and a more responsive, connected city.

🤝
Who We Serve

Through reporting and public engagement, we help residents become active participants in improving safety and quality of life — while making sure city officials are made aware of concerns in every neighborhood.

Our Commitment

This project is meant to complement and support existing city services, including EP311. Rather than replacing them, we promote community awareness and provide residents with an additional way to document and share concerns affecting their neighborhoods.

We believe that meaningful change begins with transparency and engaged residents. By working together, we can build a safer, better-maintained El Paso.
