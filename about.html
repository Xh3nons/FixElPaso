Mission

To strengthen communication between El Pasoans and local government by providing a community-driven platform for documenting and raising awareness of infrastructure concerns. We aim to help residents become active participants in improving the safety and quality of our city, while making officials aware of concerns in their communities.

Vision

An El Paso where residents and government work together to improve the city and build stronger communities. By increasing visibility into infrastructure concerns and encouraging participation, we hope to support better decision-making and contribute to a more responsive, connected city.

Commitment

This project is meant to complement existing city services, including EP311 — not replace them. We promote community awareness and give residents an additional way to document and share concerns. Meaningful change begins with transparency and engaged residents. Working together, we can build a safer, better-maintained El Paso.
